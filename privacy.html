Vishwakarma Studio — Privacy Policy Who we are. Vishwakarma Studio is made and run by Manjinder ("we"). What we collect when you register and use the app • Your name, email address, and optionally company, phone number and country, which you enter when you register. • Your password, stored only in encrypted (hashed) form by our login provider. • Your license details: plan, start and end dates, license keys you activate. • For each device you use: a random device identifier created by the app, the device type (for example "Chrome on Windows" or "Android app"), and when it was first and last used. • When you sign in, download the app and change your license (an activity log used to prevent misuse). What we do not collect. Your projects and drawings are stored on your own device and are not sent to us. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising. Why. To create and secure your account, let Manjinder approve it, check your license and device limit, prevent misuse, and contact you about your account. Where. Account data is stored with our hosting provider, Supabase (supabase.com), on servers that may be outside your country. How long. While your account exists. When an account is deleted, its details, licenses and device list are removed; the activity log may keep the email address for up to 12 months for fraud prevention. Your choices. You can see your details in the app (Help › Account & license), correct them by contacting us, and delete your account at any time with "Delete my account" in that dialog, or by contacting us.
